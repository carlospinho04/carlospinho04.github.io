Privacy Policy — Fact Scroll

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Fact Scroll is built and operated by Carlos Pinho. This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: your app data stays on your device. The only exception is advertising: if you choose to watch an ad, Google AdMob receives device data needed to serve it (see below).

What's stored on your device

The app uses Android's standard local storage (SharedPreferences) to remember:

  • Number of facts viewed today, today's date, and any extra facts you've unlocked
  • Which facts you've already seen, so they aren't repeated
  • The topics you picked as interests, and which facts you've discovered in each topic
  • Your current streak and recent activity, used for the weekly recap
  • Which facts you've liked
  • Your daily-challenge answers and score
  • Onboarding progress

This data stays on your device. It is not transmitted to us or any third party. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app offers an optional rewarded ad ("Watch an ad — 5 more facts") that unlocks extra facts. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, app version)
  • Ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was shown or watched)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Google's practices are described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, the app asks for your consent before requesting ads. You can change your choice at any time by long-pressing the "Watch an ad — 5 more facts" button and selecting Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

What we don't do

  • We do not run our own analytics or telemetry.
  • We do not have user accounts.
  • We do not collect names, emails, locations, or contacts.
  • We do not sell your data.

What the app sends to others

  • Wikipedia (when you tap "Learn more") — opens the source URL in your default browser. Wikipedia receives a standard browser request from your device. Their privacy policy applies: https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy
  • Sharing (when you tap "Share") — opens Android's share sheet with the fact or your score as an image. Whichever app you pick (Messages, WhatsApp, etc.) handles the content under its own privacy policy.

The app has no in-app purchases.

Notifications and widget

The app can schedule a single local notification at 6 PM as a streak reminder, showing the start of a fact. The optional home screen widget shows a fact of the day. Both are generated on your device from facts bundled with the app. No remote server is involved.

Children

Fact Scroll is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect, store, or process any of your data on our servers, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. For data collected by Google AdMob, use the controls described in the Advertising section or contact Google. To clear all app data on your device, uninstall the app or use Android's Settings → Apps → Fact Scroll → Storage → Clear data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same URL with a new "Last updated" date.
